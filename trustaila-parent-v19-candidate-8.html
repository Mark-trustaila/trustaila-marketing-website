ContractVault New
Contracts

ContractVault reads your signed contracts and keeps what they oblige. It extracts expiry, notice periods, renewals and the obligations in between, each with the passage it came from, for a lawyer to confirm. It counts the deadlines in working days where the contract says so, and when a date calls for action it drafts the notice and proposes it. Nothing is sent without approval.

Legal is leaving the building, the way payroll, IT and facilities did.

The routine layer, with your lawyer still in charge.

Routine legal work has stayed inside the company - until now - because the cost of the alternative was higher. An outside lawyer starts from zero on your context, bills you for briefing them, and you can't see how the work was done. So companies hire a general counsel and give them everything.

An AI system built for the job, what engineers call a harness, removes both costs. It holds your policies, history and positions, so nothing starts from zero. It logs every check, draft and sign-off, so external work becomes more visible and auditable than an employee's. Once context and control can sit outside the firm, the routine layer of the legal function can follow the other functions out of the building. AI does not just make things faster. It changes where work is best done and by whom.

The argument in full is in Reconsidering Coase in the Age of AI, a working paper by Mark Kingsley-Williams (founder and CEO of AiLA), Lucy Tallon and Mijo Juric Williams.Linked by economist Tyler Cowen on Marginal Revolution, August 2026.

Why AiLA is faster and cheaper

A law firm's hourly rate has to pay for its pyramid structure, so junior hours are billed at a mark-up, and partner profit is priced into every minute of every matter. That is the business model, and it is why the routine work, the work you send most often, is the worst value, the most money for the least judgement.

AiLA is different. AI does most of the labour, so the price of a counted deadline or a drafted notice is low. Our lawyers get involved only when a matter escalates, and then on a quoted fee. You pay a retainer against the work, as with an accountant, so every further contract costs nothing extra within your retainer. And the matters that never arrive, because they were caught, cost nothing at all.

What AiLA never does

Handing legal work to AI takes trust, so the limits are built in and every one of them can be checked.

Send or file without you

Nothing consequential leaves without your sign-off. Aila proposes and a person decides.

Guess at a date or a number

Deadlines and arithmetic come from fixed rules written for each jurisdiction. The AI reads and drafts. It never calculates.

Invent what it doesn't know

Answers are grounded in the official record and traceable to it. Where a rule isn't verified, the deadline is flagged, not guessed.

Act without a log

Every action taken on your behalf is recorded with a reason and available for review.

Train on your matters

Your data is never used to train models. All data is processed and stored in compliance with UK and EU GDPR, and where you need it AiLA runs as your own instance in your own cloud.

Leave you alone in a dispute

When it turns contentious, our lawyers take it, on a business they already know in detail across broad areas.

Questions you may have

Is AiLA a law firm?

No, and for the work it covers it doesn't need to be. The routine and contentious registry and regulatory work AiLA handles is not reserved legal activity in the UK (the work only regulated lawyers may do). Where a matter does head to court, AiLA prepares the file and works alongside your chosen representatives.

Who actually does the work?

AiLA reads the registers and the contracts, counts every date, watches for what changes and drafts what a date calls for. Aila proposes the next step. A lawyer decides in AiLA before anything changes. When a matter escalates, AiLA's lawyers take it, working with the AI rather than instead of it.

We have a legal team. Is this only for companies without one?

No. A team of five hit by a diligence request, a restructuring and a product launch in the same month has the same problem as a sole general counsel, more obligations than can be handled. AiLA takes the routine layer permanently and absorbs the surges on top, on the same retainer, so the team stops staffing for the peak. Most legal departments already buy overflow this way. This is overflow that knows your business.

Do I have to log in to keep up?

No. Aila sits in Slack or Teams, answers what is due and says each morning what has changed. Decisions are one click away in AiLA. The app is there when you want the full details, and its own inbox and your drive and calendar are coming next.

What does it cost?

Each solution is priced as a monthly retainer based on scope and volume, the way an accountant charges. Nothing is charged by seat or by the hour. Escalated matters are quoted individually before any work begins.

We're a public body. Does this work for us?

Yes. PrivacyVault is built for the request volumes and statutory deadlines that information governance teams carry, and it can run as your own instance in your own cloud.

Can we take one solution without the others?

Yes. Each solution stands alone, with its own onboarding and pricing, and the escalation promise comes with every one of them.
